Pool Chemistry Assistant Privacy Policy

Updated October 18, 2026 · Version 2.0

About this policy

This policy describes Pool Chemistry Assistant version 2.0, operated by Jeremy Gibson (JSG Systems). It is specific to this app; other JSG Systems applications may have different practices. Contact: admin.jsg.systems@gmail.com.

Local pool data

The app saves the pool profiles, settings, readings, notes, and unfinished tests you enter in its storage on your iPhone or iPad. This version has no developer-operated server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking feature, or app account. Pool entries are not automatically sent to Jeremy Gibson or JSG Systems.

Optional cloud syncing

If you choose a folder through Files, the app reads and writes shared pool settings, saved readings, notes, and change history in that folder. A randomly generated installation identifier is included to distinguish changes from different installations. Unfinished draft readings and device connection bookmarks are not shared. Your selected provider, such as iCloud Drive, stores and transfers files under its own terms and privacy policy. Anyone you allow to access that folder may read its contents. The app does not itself make folders public. Review your provider’s access and sharing settings before connecting a folder.

Import and export

Importing a supported JSON backup creates local pool data until you explicitly set up sharing. Exporting creates a snapshot at the destination you choose. Exports may include your notes and measurements. Keep those files private and avoid including sensitive personal information in pool names or notes.

Retention, recovery, and deletion

You can edit or delete readings in History and remove a pool through Manage pools. Shared change journals retain previous versions and deletion records, so removing an item from the active view does not erase all historical copies. Recovery snapshots made before upgrades, imports, and pool deletion can remain in the app’s Documents folder. Disconnecting stops subsequent sync checks but leaves local data and existing provider files. To remove remote copies and exports, use Files or your provider’s tools and address copies on other devices. Providers can retain backups and recently deleted files under their own policies. Deleting the app removes local app data; iOS backups, exports, remote folders, and copies held by others must be managed separately. Do not delete the app before updating if you want to retain local readings.

Permissions and security

The app uses app storage and access to files or folders you select. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or health data. Protect access to your device, provider account, folders, and exports. No storage or transfer system can be guaranteed completely secure. The developer cannot access, recover, or delete private provider files unless you independently supply them for support.

Support and external links

If you send an email or an attachment to support, the recipient and email providers receive the information you choose to send. Do not include sensitive information. Contact admin.jsg.systems@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests concerning support information you supplied. External help links open other websites, whose operators may process connection details under their own policies.

Children

Pool Chemistry Assistant is a pool-maintenance utility intended for people responsible for pool care, not a service directed to children. It does not have an account or a developer data-collection form. Do not enter children’s personal information in pool notes or shared files.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. For questions, contact Jeremy Gibson at admin.jsg.systems@gmail.com.
