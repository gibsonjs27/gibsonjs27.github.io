JSG Systems – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

JSG Systems (“we”, “our”, “us”) develops mobile applications including, but not limited to, THE Workout Tracker, Pool Chemistry Assistant, and any future apps published under the JSG Systems name. This Privacy Policy explains how our apps handle your information, what data is collected, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding your privacy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do not sell your data, we do not store personal information on our servers, and we design our apps to collect as little information as possible.

1. Information We Collect

a. Device Information (Non‑Personal)

Our apps may collect basic, non‑personal device information such as: device model, operating system version, app version, and anonymous usage statistics. This information helps us improve app performance, stability, and user experience.

b. App Usage Data (Non‑Personal)

Some apps may collect anonymous usage metrics such as feature usage, crash reports, and interaction patterns. This data is strictly non‑identifiable and used only to improve app functionality.

c. User‑Provided Data

Some apps allow you to enter information (e.g., workout logs, pool chemistry readings). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to JSG Systems. We do not store, transmit, or access your personal entries.

2. Advertising & Third‑Party Services

Some JSG Systems apps may use third‑party advertising platforms such as Google AdMob. These services may collect anonymous device identifiers, ad interaction data, approximate location (non‑GPS), and analytics related to ad performance.

We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

All collection is handled by the third‑party provider under their own privacy policies. You can review Google’s policies here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

For iOS users, apps that use advertising identifiers will request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework.

  • You may choose Allow Tracking or Ask App Not to Track.
  • Your choice is fully respected.
  • If you decline, your data will not be used for cross‑app tracking or personalized advertising.

You can change this setting anytime in your device’s Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

4. Data Storage

JSG Systems does not store personal data on external servers, maintain cloud databases of user information, or collect personally identifiable information.

All user‑generated content remains local to your device unless you explicitly back it up using your own platform tools (e.g., iCloud, Google Drive).

5. Data Sharing & Selling

JSG Systems does not sell your data, share your data with third parties, or transfer personal information to advertisers or partners.

The only data accessible to third parties is anonymous, non‑personal information collected by services like AdMob, as described above.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our apps are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information within one of our apps, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes occur, the “Effective Date” will be updated, and the revised policy will be published at: https://gibsonjs27.github.io/privacy.html.
